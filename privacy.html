RONIN — Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026. About RONIN

RONIN is a personal assistant written and run by one person, for their own use. It is not a service offered to the public. This policy describes what it does with the Google account of the person who runs it.

What it accesses

When its owner signs in with Google, RONIN asks for:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): sender, recipients, subject, date, labels and the short preview snippet of each message, to work out which conversations are waiting on a reply.
  • Gmail, modify (gmail.modify), only if the owner switches the feature on: to move old bulk mail (newsletters and notifications) to Gmail's Trash after the owner approves each batch. RONIN never permanently deletes mail and never empties the Trash.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly), only if the owner switches the feature on: event titles and times, to notice clashes and invitations awaiting a reply. Event descriptions and attendee lists are not requested.

What it does not do with mail

  • Messages that look sensitive — password resets, one-time codes, security notices, banking — are recorded by metadata only; their content is not stored.
  • RONIN does not send, reply to, or forward email, and does not change anything in a mailbox other than moving approved bulk mail to Trash.

Where the data is kept

  • In a private database in the owner's own account with Supabase (a hosted database provider), and on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • There is no shared database, and nobody other than the owner has an account on it.

Who it is shared with

With no one for their own purposes. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to train models. Two service providers process it on the owner's behalf, each under the owner's own account:

  • Supabase hosts the database described above.
  • Microsoft Azure OpenAI processes text from the owner's mail only if the owner switches on the "ask" feature: short passages relevant to a question the owner has typed or spoken are sent to the owner's own Azure OpenAI resource to produce an answer.

That processing is governed by the owner's agreements with those providers, and RONIN uses the data for nothing else.

Limited Use

RONIN's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information obtained from Gmail is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above, and is not transferred to others except as described here, nor read by humans except with the owner's consent or as required by law.

Keeping and deleting it

  • Data is kept for as long as the owner uses RONIN, and can be removed at any time.
  • To stop all access: remove RONIN at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access ends immediately.
  • To delete what RONIN holds: the owner deletes the rows in their own database. Nothing is held elsewhere.
